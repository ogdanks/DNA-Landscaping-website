Professional Stone Masonry in Central Massachusetts

For over 30 years, DNA Landscaping has been the trusted name for expert stone masonry throughout Westborough and Central Massachusetts. Our skilled masons combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques to create stunning, durable stonework that enhances your property's beauty and value.

Whether you need a new stone foundation, a decorative garden wall, or natural stone steps and walkways, we bring precision and artistry to every project. We work with natural stone, fieldstone, granite, bluestone, and other premium materials to match your vision and architectural style.

Our Stone Masonry Services

Stone Foundations

New construction, repairs, and restoration of stone foundations for homes, barns, and outbuildings. We specialize in matching existing stonework for seamless repairs.

  • Foundation construction & reinforcement
  • Historic foundation restoration
  • Stone basement walls
  • Crack repair & repointing

Garden & Retaining Walls

Beautiful, functional stone walls that define spaces, prevent erosion, and add character to your landscape. Built for strength and lasting beauty.

  • Dry-stack stone walls
  • Mortared stone walls
  • Terraced garden walls
  • Fieldstone & natural stone

Stone Steps & Walkways

Durable, beautiful stone steps and walkways that provide safe, elegant access while enhancing the natural flow of your landscape.

  • Natural stone steps

  • Stone landings & entryways
  • Custom stone treads

Decorative Stonework

Add elegance and character with custom stone features designed to complement your home and landscape.

  • Stone columns & pillars

  • Custom stone features
