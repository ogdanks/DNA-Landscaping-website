Large-Scale Privacy Hedge Specialists

Privacy hedges are a beautiful, natural alternative to fencing — but they require expert care to maintain their shape, health, and screening ability. DNA Landscaping specializes in the precision trimming and sculpting of large-scale privacy hedges throughout Central Massachusetts.

Whether you have towering arborvitae, dense evergreen screens, or formal hedge rows, our experienced team has the equipment and expertise to shape them perfectly. We understand hedge growth patterns, seasonal trimming schedules, and the techniques needed to promote thick, healthy growth from top to bottom.
