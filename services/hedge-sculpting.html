Precision Pruning

The art of the clean line. Expertly crafted pruning that enhances the structure, health, and curated beauty of your entire landscape.

Refinement in Every Cut

A world-class landscape is defined by its details. Beyond simple maintenance, Precision Pruning is about vision. It is the practice of shaping a landscape to its highest potential—creating balance, revealing hidden forms, and maintaining the sharp geometry that defines a premium property.

At DNA Landscaping, we bring over 30 years of experience to every specimen we touch. Whether we are hand-shaping a privacy hedge, thinning an ornamental tree, or restoring a foundation planting, our goal is a landscape that feels intentional, architectural, and immaculately clean.

Service Specialties

Structural Living Walls

Precision shaping for large-scale privacy hedges, from arborvitae to hemlock. We create perfectly level tops and plumb faces that act as the structural "walls" of your outdoor rooms.

Artful Ornamental Care

Detailed hand-pruning for Japanese Maples, Boxwoods, Hydrangeas, and Specimen shrubs. We focus on enhancing the natural character and airflow of each plant.

Small Tree Refinement

Elevating the canopy and thinning the interior of ornamental trees. This improves light penetration for ground cover and creates a safer, more wind-resistant structure.

Estate Restoration

Patient, multi-season pruning plans to reclaim overgrown landscapes. We bring back the original design intent of old-growth plantings without shocking the plant.

The DNA Approach to Precision

The Editorial Eye

We don't just cut—we edit. Every pass is calculated to remove conflict, improve view-lines, and ensure the surrounding landscape is complemented by the work.

Long-Term Structural Integrity

Our pruning supports the life of the plant. We use specialized thinning techniques that prevent interior dieback and heavy snow damage, ensuring your investment grows more beautiful with age.

Consistent Perspective

For long hedge runs and formal alignments, we use precise sighting and guide-lines to ensure a result that is visually perfect from every vantage point on the estate.
