Professional Lawn Care for Central Massachusetts

A healthy, vibrant lawn doesn't happen by accident — it requires consistent care, proper timing, and the right treatments for New England's unique climate. DNA Landscaping provides complete lawn care services designed to keep your grass thick, green, and resilient through all four seasons.

From weekly mowing and edging to seasonal aeration and treatments, we handle every aspect of lawn maintenance so you can enjoy a beautiful yard without the work.

Our Lawn Care Services

Regular Mowing & Edging

Consistent, professional mowing keeps your lawn healthy and attractive. We adjust cutting height seasonally and edge all borders for a clean, manicured look.

  • Weekly or bi-weekly mowing
  • Seasonal height adjustments
  • Precision edging & trimming
  • Grass clipping management

Aeration & Overseeding

Core aeration relieves soil compaction and overseeding fills in thin areas, promoting thick, healthy turf that crowds out weeds naturally.

  • Fall core aeration
  • Premium grass seed selection
  • Overseeding for thick turf
  • New lawn establishment

Seasonal Cleanup

Prepare your lawn for success with thorough spring and fall cleanups that remove debris, promote growth, and protect against winter damage.

  • Spring cleanup & dethatching
  • Fall leaf removal
  • Bed clearing & mulching
  • Storm debris removal

Lawn Treatments

Targeted treatments to promote healthy growth, control weeds, and protect against pests and disease throughout the growing season.

  • Fertilization programs
  • Weed control & prevention
  • Disease management
  • Soil pH adjustment
