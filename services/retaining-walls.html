Retaining Walls

Engineered retaining walls that prevent erosion while adding structure and beauty to your landscape.

Expert Retaining Wall Construction

Retaining walls are essential for managing sloped landscapes, preventing soil erosion, and creating level outdoor spaces. DNA Landscaping designs and builds structurally sound, attractive retaining walls that solve practical problems while enhancing your property's beauty.

Whether you need a simple garden wall or a complex tiered system, we have the engineering knowledge and construction expertise to build retaining walls that last for generations.

Our Retaining Wall Services

Natural Stone Walls

Timeless beauty meets structural integrity with natural stone retaining walls. Each wall is custom-built to match your landscape.

  • Fieldstone retaining walls
  • Granite & bluestone walls
  • Dry-stack construction
  • Mortared stone walls

Segmented Block Walls

Engineered block systems provide maximum strength and versatility with a variety of colors, textures, and styles to choose from.

Tiered Garden Beds

Erosion Control

  • Drainage system design
  • Grading & excavation
  • Long-term erosion prevention
